# QuantFlow Labs

Welcome to **QuantFlow Labs**. This organization serves as a development hub for high-performance trading software, specialized libraries, and experimental algorithmic components.

Our core focus is bridging the gap between mathematical theory and market execution, leveraging the speed of **modern C++** and the native capabilities of **MQL5**.

---

## 🔬 What We Do

We develop modular, optimized, and experimental infrastructure for financial markets. Most of our work centers around extending trading platforms and building standalone analytical tools.

* **Modern MQL5 Development:** Next-gen MetaTrader 5 libraries, custom indicators, and automated execution scripts.
* **C++ DLL Extensions:** High-speed computation modules designed to offload heavy calculations from the trading terminal.
* **Standalone Applications:** Independent software for market data analysis, advanced charting, and mathematical modeling.
* **Experimental R&D:** Prototyping analytical concepts, volume profiles, and statistical filters.
